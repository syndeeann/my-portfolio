I · QUEUE

A legend is chosen

Each story begins as a short entry in a queue: the creature or legend, a premise, a twist idea and a target length.

YAML queue
II · SCRIPT

Claude writes the tale

Claude writes a Tagalog horror script in short beats, each sized to one clip: a hook, a slow build, and a twist ending.

Claude
III · PROMPTS

One prompt per shot

Every beat becomes a 9:16 video prompt with the same foggy, moonlit look and a hushed Tagalog narrator, plus a caption and hashtags.

Prompt engineering
IV · GENERATE

Google Flow films it

Clips are generated in Google Flow with native narration, while a credit budget decides how many stories can be made that day.

Google Flow · Veo
V · EDIT

Stitched into one reel

The clips are joined into one vertical video, small enough for a quick upload from a phone, and polished for short-form viewing.

CapCut · ffmpeg
VI · APPROVE

Nothing escapes without me

Nothing posts on its own. I review every video and caption, then approve it, ask for a clip to be redone, or reject it.

Human review
