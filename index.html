02 · Script

Claude writes the story

Claude writes a Tagalog horror script in short beats, each sized to one clip: a hook, a slow build, and a twist ending.

Claude
03 · Prompts

One prompt per shot

Every beat becomes a 9:16 video prompt with a consistent look and a hushed Tagalog narrator, plus a ready-to-post caption and hashtags.

Prompt engineering
04 · Generate

Google Flow films it

Clips are generated in Google Flow with native narration, while a credit budget decides how many stories can be made that day.

Google Flow · Veo

Cut into one reel

The clips are joined into a single vertical video, sized for quick upload from a phone, and polished for short-form viewing.

CapCut · ffmpeg

Nothing goes out automatically. I review each video and caption, then approve it, ask for a clip to be redone, or reject it before posting.
